You are the production planner of a small food factory. Build a schedule for the 4 jobs below on two machines, A and B.

HARD RULES (every one of them must hold):
1. Each job runs exactly once, on exactly one machine (A or B), without interruption.
2. A machine runs one job at a time. Processing times depend on the machine (table below).
3. Changeover: if job Y runs right after job X on the same machine, Y may not start until at least the changeover time from X to Y (table below) has passed since X finished. The first job on a machine needs no changeover. The two machines are independent of each other.
4. Deadline: every job must finish at or before its deadline.
5. Allergens: on each machine, every job that contains nuts must come after all jobs that do not contain nuts.
6. Machine B is shut down for power saving from minute 40 to minute 70. No job may be running on B during that window (a job may finish exactly at minute 40 or start exactly at minute 70).
7. Time starts at minute 0. All times are whole minutes.

OBJECTIVE: minimize  cost = makespan + running cost.
Makespan is the minute at which the last job finishes. Running cost is 1 per minute of processing on machine A plus 3 per minute of processing on machine B.

JOBS:
job | minutes on A | minutes on B | deadline | contains nuts
P1 | 50 | 35 | 185 | no
P2 | 50 | 35 | 125 | yes
P3 | 50 | 35 | 325 | no
P4 | 35 | 25 | 295 | no

CHANGEOVER MINUTES (row = job that just finished, column = job that starts next):
     | P1 | P2 | P3 | P4
P1 | - | 5 | 5 | 25
P2 | 15 | - | 15 | 20
P3 | 15 | 15 | - | 25
P4 | 25 | 25 | 15 | -

You may use code execution or any tool you have to solve the problem and to verify your answer.

OUTPUT: finish your answer with one JSON object in exactly this shape, with nothing after it:
{"schedule": [{"job": "P1", "machine": "A", "start": 0}, {"job": "P2", "machine": "B", "start": 0}]}
List every job exactly once. "start" is the start minute (a whole number).
